Set up Streamer.bot

  1. Click Copy import code.
  2. In Streamer.bot open Import (top bar), paste, and click Import.
  3. Come back here and click Check again.

You still have the old Printer Bot | Events and Printer Bot | Print Routine actions. After importing, disable or delete both, otherwise every event prints twice.

This dock is loaded from a web server but talks only to Streamer.bot on this computer. If your browser asks whether this site may connect to devices on your computer or local network (it may be called "Apps on device"), choose Allow.
